About the Painting

The Brooklyn Bridge has long stood as one of New York City's most enduring symbols of ambition, resilience, and connection. In this expressive interpretation, Kenneth J. Lewis Sr. captures more than the architectural landmark itself—he conveys its energy, rhythm, and presence through bold brushwork, layered texture, and vibrant color.

Rather than striving for photographic precision, the painting embraces movement and emotion. Rich blues and turquoise waters contrast with the warm golden tones of the bridge's stone towers, while expressive marks and visible brushstrokes create a sense of atmosphere and life. The surrounding skyline emerges through gestural layers of paint, allowing the bridge to remain the commanding focal point.

Executed in oil on canvas, the painting celebrates the tactile qualities of the medium. Thick passages of paint, varied textures, and confident application invite the viewer to experience the work both from a distance and up close, where each brushstroke contributes to the overall composition.

This original work reflects Kenneth J. Lewis Sr.'s distinctive contemporary style—balancing recognizable architecture with expressive abstraction to create a painting that is both timeless and deeply personal. Brooklyn Bridge is a statement piece designed for collectors who appreciate original fine art, strong composition, and the enduring character of one of America's most iconic landmarks.
