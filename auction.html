About the Luggage Series

A vibrant celebration of color, form, memory, and travel, the Luggage Series transforms the familiar shape and symbolism of luggage into bold abstract compositions. Each work is filled with movement, layered marks, expressive color, and the suggestion of stories carried from one place to another.

Luggage holds more than personal belongings. It carries evidence of where we have been, what we have survived, what we have left behind, and what we hope to discover. Every journey adds another layer to the traveler, just as every mark and passage of color adds another layer to these paintings.

The series does not present luggage through literal realism. Instead, the subject is reconstructed through color, line, gesture, shape, interruption, and repetition. Forms appear, disappear, overlap, and reemerge, creating paintings that feel active, personal, and continually in motion.

About Luggage No. 14

Luggage No. 14 is built around a powerful combination of red, orange, yellow, black, pink, brown, and touches of pale blue. The composition carries an immediate heat and intensity, creating a work that commands attention from across a room while rewarding close viewing.

Beneath the energetic surface are shapes that suggest bags, handles, straps, compartments, labels, pathways, and objects packed together. These forms are never completely fixed. They shift between recognition and abstraction, allowing each viewer to enter the work through their own experiences and memories.

Sweeping lines move throughout the painting like routes on a map. Some marks circle back upon themselves, while others cross, connect, separate, and continue forward. The painting becomes both a collection of objects and a record of movement.

The red-dominant palette gives Luggage No. 14 a sense of urgency, passion, activity, and life. Dark passages create depth and structure, while yellow, orange, pink, and lighter marks rise from the surface like memories, destinations, conversations, and fragments of experience.

Artistic significance: Luggage No. 14 reflects the emotional weight of what people carry with them—the visible, the hidden, the remembered, and the still unfolding. The painting is both a celebration of travel and a meditation on the personal history contained within every journey.
Surface, Scale and Material

The painting was created on architectural panel, a rigid surface that supports extensive layering, pressure, scraping, drawing, repainting, and direct mark making. Unlike stretched canvas, the solid panel allows the artist to work aggressively across the surface without losing the physical character of each gesture.

At 44 × 60 inches, the work has substantial presence without becoming difficult to place. The horizontal format allows the painting to anchor a living room, dining area, office, conference room, reception area, private gallery, or large interior wall.

The painting can be viewed from a distance as one large field of movement and color. At closer range, individual marks, layered passages, drawn lines, interruptions, repeated forms, and changes in texture become increasingly visible.

Custom framing included: The successful purchaser will receive Luggage No. 14 installed in a custom black float frame. The float presentation will create a narrow visual separation between the artwork and the outer frame, allowing the edges and full surface of the architectural panel to remain visible. The frame cost is included in the winning purchase price.
Kenneth J. Lewis Sr.'s Artistic Approach

Kenneth J. Lewis Sr. is a self-taught contemporary artist known for expressive brushwork, visible texture, spontaneous line, layered color, and direct emotional mark making. His paintings preserve the evidence of their creation rather than hiding the movement of the artist's hand.

In Luggage No. 14, painted passages interact with drawn and gestural marks to create a surface that feels discovered rather than mechanically planned. The composition develops through repetition, response, intuition, adjustment, and the willingness to allow one mark to influence the next.

The work reflects the artist's belief that the brushstroke should not be hidden. Each line, scrape, painted shape, and irregular passage remains part of the final experience of the painting.

Placement and Collection

Luggage No. 14 is suited for a private residence, corporate office, executive suite, gallery, hotel, design collection, cultural institution, law office, reception area, restaurant, creative workspace, or serious contemporary art collection.

Its strong colors make it especially effective within interiors featuring neutral walls, black accents, natural wood, leather, mid-century modern furniture, contemporary furnishings, or architectural materials such as concrete, stone, glass, and steel.

As part of an ongoing numbered series, the work also offers collectors an opportunity to acquire a distinct chapter within a larger artistic exploration of travel, memory, movement, and the emotional weight of what people carry.
