Featured collection · Seven original paintings

The Color of Jazz

Bring the whole ensemble home. Seven original oil paintings on canvas by Kenneth J. Lewis Sr. celebrate the soul of jazz through bold color, expressive brushwork, and the atmosphere of vintage clubs and city streets. Trombone, bass, trumpet, voice, saxophone, drums, and piano each take a turn in the spotlight.

Collected together, these paintings create a coordinated statement for a home, music room, restaurant, hotel, or corporate collection. Display them as one ensemble or carry their shared rhythm through several connected spaces.
