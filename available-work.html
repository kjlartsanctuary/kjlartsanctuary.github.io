Featured collection · Four original oil paintings on canvas

Dressed Between Times

Fashion crosses centuries, and time takes a human form. In this imaginative collection by Kenneth J. Lewis Sr., clock-headed figures combine clothing from different eras to explore identity, memory, and the way the past remains part of the present.

Edwardian tailoring meets seventies bell-bottoms, Mod geometry meets Romantic volume, and a gentleman brings formal tradition into the modern day. Expressive brushwork and distinctive backgrounds connect these figures while giving each an individual personality.

Collect the four paintings together for a striking display in a home, office, boutique, or hospitality setting, or select an individual work that speaks to your own sense of style and time.
